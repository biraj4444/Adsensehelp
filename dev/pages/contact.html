Quick answers

Can you get my AdSense application approved?

No. Only Google decides, and nobody outside Google can promise a result. What I can do is help you understand the setup and fix technical mistakes.

Is my message stored on this website?

No. The site is static and has no server or database. The message only exists in your email app and with your email provider.

I found a mistake. What is the best way to report it?

Choose "Something is wrong or out of date", name the page and, if you can, paste the link to Google's page that says otherwise. Corrections are very welcome.

How quickly will I get a reply?

I read every message and reply as soon as I can, but I cannot promise a fixed time.
